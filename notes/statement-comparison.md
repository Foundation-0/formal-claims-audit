# Statement comparison protocol

The independent reader derives the intended statement from the paper before reading the lab formalization. Freeze that artifact and its author record. The input recorder hashes both statements but does not compare them.

Replay the pinned builds and axiom extraction first. Then use a bridge lemma in each relevant implication direction or a compatible Comparator version. Record the precise arithmetic assumptions, quantifiers, domains, asymptotic constants and exceptional cases. A successful build alone says nothing about fidelity.

Only a justified comparison produces a verdicts/ JSON file. Non-faithful verdicts require qualified human review before external reporting. No placeholder verdict is committed by this change.
